feat: Refine Waybar styling and camera icon

This commit refines Waybar styling and updates the camera icon.

Key changes include:
- Updated the camera off icon to `\udb86\uddbf` (U+E67F) in both main Waybar and Waybar Control Center configurations.
- Reverted the `format-muted` change in the `pulseaudio` module, removing the `---` placeholder.
- Reverted `min-width` from the `.module` class in `style.css`.
- Added `min-width: 50px;` to `#pulseaudio`, `#battery`, `#cpu`, `#memory`, and `#network` modules in `style.css` to prevent layout shifts when percentage values change.
